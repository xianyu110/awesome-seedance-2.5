THE WOMAN: late 20s, long dark hair, deep red silk evening dress,
barefoot. Calm, unafraid.
THE CREATURE (original design): SHE IS THE CREATURE. Not a separate
being. Her own body grows and armors. Obsidian-black interlocking plates
sheathing her limbs and torso, molten orange fissures glowing between
every plate, elongated arms, a crown of jagged back-swept horns, amber
eyes. Human posture and proportions kept, scaled to 90 metres.
TRANSFORMATION PHASES, in strict order:
P1 hairline fractures of orange light open across her skin.
P2 the fissures widen, embers pour upward off her shoulders.
P3 obsidian plates surface through her skin and lock into place, dress
burning away into ash.
P4 she grows upward and outward from her own standing body, feet planted,
weight driving into the ground, horns pushing through last.
LOCATION: dense modern city, wide granite plaza, glass towers all sides,
dusk, empty streets, abandoned cars, streetlights flickering on.
COLOR 60:30:10 — 60% cool blue-grey dusk glass and stone / 30% deep
shadow / 10% molten orange fissures and embers.
