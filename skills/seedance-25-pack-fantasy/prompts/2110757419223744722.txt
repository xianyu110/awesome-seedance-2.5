SCENE CONTEXT
In Glimmer Reef, Tuck — a small stylized hermit crab who has outgrown his old shell — searches the spilled cargo of a sunken rowboat for a new home.
He tries three human objects as shells.
Every try fails in a different way.
TOO BIG.
TOO HEAVY.
SEE-THROUGH.
Defeated, he loses hope.
Then a glint leads him to a beautiful pearl shell, hidden at the back of the reef behind the sunken boat.
It is EXACTLY his size.
He backs into its opening TAIL-FIRST, WEARS it on his back,
walks happily past the empty glass jar that once embarrassed him,
and celebrates: "Perfect fit!"
34 seconds total.
16:9 widescreen.
Bright animated-feature underwater daylight.
Fast comedy timing.
Large readable expressions.
Strong physical weight.
Tender emotional final beat.
