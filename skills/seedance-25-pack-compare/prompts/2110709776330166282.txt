Spike Lee double dolly shot. Night, an old street. A middle-aged man in a dark coat stands on an invisible moving platform: feet still, body not bobbing, gliding toward the camera at a steady speed, as if floating.

The camera dollies backward at exactly the same speed, so he stays the same size in frame while the street, streetlights and passers-by keep flowing backward behind him. He looks straight ahead, calm. Medium shot, frontal, 35mm, warm streetlights, shallow depth of field.
