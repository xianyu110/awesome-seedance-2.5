SETTING

A quiet old-fashioned café at sunset. Warm orange sunlight pours through the large windows. Vintage décor, wooden tables, soft curtains moving in the breeze, only a few distant customers. The entire story takes place in this single room.

The scene begins with a wide establishing shot. Adrian sits alone at a table, nervously stirring untouched coffee. Evelyn enters the café and notices him. The camera cuts between Adrian's hopeful eyes and Evelyn's uncertain expression before she slowly walks toward him.

She quietly asks, "May I?"

Adrian nods. She sits across from him.

The tension is immediate.

Alternating over-the-shoulder shots reveal neither knows how to begin. Close-ups linger on nervous fingers, subtle eye movements, hesitant smiles, cups of coffee left untouched, and tiny changes in expression.

A slow push-in captures Adrian finally breaking the silence.

"I wasn't sure you'd come."

Evelyn lowers her eyes before softly answering,

"I almost didn't."

Silence.

The camera slowly circles around them as neither looks away.

Close-up on Adrian gathering the courage to reach across the table. His hand stops halfway.

Evelyn notices.

She hesitates...

...then gently places her hand into his.

Extreme close-up of their hands touching.

Neither speaks.

A tear quietly forms in Evelyn's eye, but she smiles.

Adrian smiles back with visible relief.

The surrounding café ambience seems to fade, leaving only their breathing.

The camera alternates between intimate close-ups, profile shots through the café window, low-angle table shots, and soft side tracking shots, emphasizing every glance and every pause.

In the final moments, they continue holding hands across the table without saying another word. The camera slowly dollies backward, revealing the warm café around them while the golden sunset fills the room. They never break eye contact.
