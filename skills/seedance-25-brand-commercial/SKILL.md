# Seedance 2.5 · Brand / Commercial Film

Longer narrative ads (~1–2 min) with **vehicle motion, night plates, premium grade**.

## Structure

| Act | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–10s | Threat or VIP detail |
| Procedure | mid | Convoy / escort / protocol |
| Arrival | late | Safe handoff / hotel / logo breath |

## Prompt cues

- Night city practicals, headlight bloom controlled
- Readable vehicle speed (not teleport)
- Logo-safe negative space in final 3s
- Voiceover optional; otherwise score + radio chatter

## Note

Seedance 2.5 single passes are often ≤30s — for ~2 min brand films, storyboard multi-clip and stitch, or use a platform that chains clips (state this to the user).
