```text
🌊 30-Second POV: Hidden Ocean Kingdom

A rider dives into a crystal-clear underwater world on a massive pearl-scaled sea dragon.

🪸 Coral reefs & thousands of fish
🏛️ Ancient underwater ruins
🐋 A gigantic peaceful sea creature
✨ Glowing particles and magical depths

Photorealistic POV, realistic underwater motion, sunlight rays, bubbles, natural ambience, breathing, whale-like calls, and cinematic music.
```
