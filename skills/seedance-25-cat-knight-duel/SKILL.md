## Pattern to reuse

1. Two character cards with strict scale relationship
2. 30s hard duration
3. Micro → mid → clash → charge → payoff shot list
4. Audio: wind, steel-on-scale, roar, energy gather, orchestra — no dialogue

## Adaptation skeleton

```text
MODEL: Seedance 2.5 | 30s | 16:9 | photoreal fantasy CG
@image1 tiny animal knight with real fur proportions + one physical sword
@image2 huge dragon with heavy scales
ENVIRONMENT: open grassland, cool-to-gold light arc across fight
SHOTS: sword CU → eye reflection → claw block → dodge sweep → failed leap slash → charge → golden burst → dragon falls
AUDIO: layered SFX + score, no dialogue/subs
```
